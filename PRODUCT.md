# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Laravel 12 (PHP 8.2+), Inertia.js 2, Vue 3 (Composition API, TypeScript), Tailwind CSS 4, Reka UI, SQLite.

## Users

1. **Academic Evaluators & Viva Examiners:** Primary audience reviewing the system during college project demonstrations and viva examinations. They evaluate architectural depth, data consistency, UI craft, and demonstrable business lifecycle flows.
2. **Rental Customers:** Individuals and executives booking premium and luxury vehicles for business or leisure travel.
3. **Fleet Operations & Dispatch Managers:** Admin staff managing vehicle availability, handover dispatch, return inspections, damage deposit settlements, and maintenance.

## Product Purpose

"Real Rent Car" is an enterprise-grade luxury vehicle rental and fleet management platform designed to deliver an end-to-end, frictionless digital automotive mobility experience. It automates every stage of the rental lifecycle—from real-time conflict-free date reservations and verified driver KYC to digital rental agreements, interactive vehicle damage inspection blueprints, and a 4-tier customer loyalty rewards program.

## Positioning

Unlike basic CRUD rental clones with static forms, Real Rent Car is a full automotive operating system featuring real-time collision prevention, prorated trip extensions, interactive SVG damage condition mapping with security deposit settlement, and an integrated Academic Presenter Suite for live evaluator demonstrations.

## Operating Context

- High-touch desktop and mobile web experiences.
- Airport and regional hub vehicle pickup/dropoff scenarios.
- Admin dispatch center with live fleet telemetry radar and turnaround queues.
- Real-time viva presentation mode (`/presentation` and floating presenter bar).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Collision-Free Booking Engine:** 2-month range calendar with atomic locking and overlap prevention.
- **Dynamic Pricing & Extras:** Daily rates, rental add-ons (GPS, child seat, roadside assist), promo coupon engine, and VIP loyalty tier discounts (Bronze 0%, Silver 5%, Gold 10%, Platinum 15%).
- **Digital Rental Legal Suite:** A4 printable rental agreements, public QR mobile gate pass, and return damage inspection receipts.
- **Damage Blueprint & Deposit Settlement:** Click-to-pin SVG vehicle blueprint deducting repair charges directly from the ₹5,000 security deposit.
- **Presenter Mode:** 1-click persona switching (Admin/Client), 8 interactive demo scenarios, and viva statistics radar.

## Brand Commitments

- **Aesthetic:** "Nordic Precision / Polestar Luxury" — Matte obsidian voids (`#06090E`), architectural titanium grounds (`#F8F9FA`), razor-sharp hairline borders, luminous ice-blue telemetry accents (`#38BDF8`), and zero generic AI slop.
- **Typography:** Swiss geometric display headings with tight tracking, monospace tabular telemetry numbers for prices and timestamps.
- **Tone:** Calm, technical, authoritative, and friction-free.

## Evidence on Hand

- SVG blueprints and regional hub radar.

## Product Principles

1. **Uncompromising Craft:** Every surface looks custom-engineered for high-end automotive hardware; no generic template feel.
2. **Deterministic Clarity:** Financial calculations, damage deductions, and loyalty tier discounts are mathematically transparent and immediately auditable.
3. **Friction-Free Handover:** The customer journey from fleet discovery to verified digital pass takes less than 60 seconds.
